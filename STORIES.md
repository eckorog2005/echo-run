# Player Stories

1. **First run.** A new player opens the page, reads the four-line legend, presses Daily run, and dies to echo #3 on round 4. The death card names the echo that got them, highlights its route in red, and shows a share line they can copy.
2. **Second try today.** The same player taps Practice daily. The HUD chip reads "practice", and the card says Practice run so they know their saved score didn't change.
3. **Learning the routes.** A player watches the replay, sees how their wide loop on round 2 became the echo that boxed them in, and tries tighter routes in Endless.
4. **The eraser decision.** On round 5 an eraser appears off to the side. Taking it costs a second and a longer route, but removes echo #1, which crosses the center.
5. **Phone play.** A player on a phone drags anywhere to steer. Keyboard hints are hidden, and the arena letterboxes to fit.
6. **Sharing.** A player pastes `Echo Run 2026-10-02 · 14 orbs · 2 erased · caught by #9` into a group chat, and friends play the same course.
